The Problem

India's healthcare system leaves hundreds of millions behind — especially in rural and semi-urban Bharat.

🏥

1 Doctor per 1,456 People

WHO recommends 1:1000. India's rural areas face acute doctor shortages — patients travel 30+ km for basic consultation.

🌐

Language Barrier

90% of health apps are English-only. 57% of Indians speak regional languages (Hindi, Odia) — they're completely excluded from digital health.

💸

Catastrophic Drug Costs

63 million Indians fall into poverty annually due to out-of-pocket healthcare expenses. Branded medicines cost 3–20x more than generics.

📋

Zero Health Records

Most rural families have no longitudinal health data. Prescriptions are lost, test results untracked, and preventive care is nonexistent.

8 ML Disease Predictors

Explainable, deterministic clinical heuristic scoring — every prediction comes with flagged conditions, risk level, and recommended action.

🩺Anemia Check
🦋Thyroid

📊 How It Works

User inputs clinical values (glucose, BP, cholesterol, BMI, age) → Deterministic rule engine scores risk 0–100% → Returns risk level (Low / Moderate / High / Critical) + flagged conditions + recommended action.

📷 AI Image Scanner

On-device TensorFlow.js MobileNet CNN classifies uploaded medical images (skin lesions, X-rays). Runs entirely in the browser — zero data leaves the phone.

✅ Explainable AI

Every prediction shows exactly which factors triggered the risk score — no black-box models. Patients understand why they're at risk.
